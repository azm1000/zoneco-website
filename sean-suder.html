Background

Sean founded ZoneCo in 2020 after founding and leading two zoning consulting practices within large law firms. From 2010 to 2014 he served as Chief Counsel of Land Use and Planning for the City of Cincinnati, representing the municipal corporation and its officials in all planning, land use, zoning, historic preservation, and building matters. He was lead counsel for the award-winning Cincinnati Form-Based Code and Historic Preservation Ordinance.

He began his career in project management for one of the largest privately held construction management companies in the country. As a lawyer and city planner with 25 years in the built environment, he is a sought-after zoning and development consultant, and has been involved in some of the most complex developments in the United States.

Teaching, speaking, and writing

Sean is a frequent speaker and panelist on zoning, zoning history, land use law, and historic preservation law, and has presented at more than 30 APA conferences. In 2026 he gave the Bettman Lecture on 100 Years of Euclid v. Ambler at the APA National Planning Conference in Detroit. He is authoring a paper, "All Zoning is New York City Zoning: How New York City Shaped Communities Across America Since 1916," to be presented at the 21st National Conference on Planning History, October 15 to 18, 2026, at the University of Cincinnati.

He has served as Ohio faculty for the Form-Based Code Institute (201 level) and as a member of the FBCI Resource Council. He is an adjunct professor of land use law at the University of Cincinnati College of Law and has guest lectured at UC's College of Design, Art, Architecture and Planning and at Miami University. He serves on the Advisory Board of the APA Planning & Law Division Case Law Digest.

His articles have appeared in Planning Magazine, the Cincinnati Enquirer, Midwest Real Estate News, Cities & Villages Magazine, and Ohio Planners News. He has been quoted extensively, including in Governing Magazine, the Cincinnati Enquirer, and the Cincinnati Business Courier.

Law practice

In addition to his work with ZoneCo, Sean is the founding partner of Suder, LLC, recognized as a Best Law Firm by Best Lawyers and a Chambers Spotlight firm for real estate in Ohio. He has been ranked Band 1 by Chambers USA in Ohio for Real Estate: Zoning/Land Use every year from 2019 to 2026, one of eight attorneys in the state and the only one in Southwest Ohio. He is licensed to practice law in Ohio, Kentucky, and the District of Columbia.

Political integrity policy

ZoneCo believes in fair, accessible, and transparent government for all. ZoneCo does not make contributions to any political party, campaign, or candidate. We would rather influence our leaders through the power of big ideas. Read the policy

Although some ZoneCo professionals are also attorneys who may be separately engaged to provide legal representation in states where we are licensed to practice law, ZoneCo is not a law firm. ZoneCo professionals do not provide legal representation or services and are not engaged in the practice of law in any jurisdiction. Engaging ZoneCo does not form an attorney-client relationship and, as such, the protections of the attorney-client relationship do not apply. If you wish to create an attorney-client relationship, you are encouraged to contact an attorney of your choosing.
