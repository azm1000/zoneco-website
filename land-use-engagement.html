Services / Development and land use engagement

From civic engagement to civic empowerment.

We only do engagement for land use and development capacities, which means we know exactly which conversations, formats, and tools actually work when the subject is form, density, and use rather than broad visions and aspirations.

Plan your engagement

A shared language

Land use and development is technical, and too often that complexity quietly shuts people out. We start by building a shared language: translating code into plain terms, and translating community values into the rules that shape what gets built. Our engagement methods are designed specifically for development and land use questions, from hands-on scenario exercises to visual tools that show what a regulation looks like on a real block.

Tools that stay behind

And we don't just run a process and leave. Every engagement produces a community toolkit your residents, staff, and officials can keep using long after adoption, so the next rezoning, potential development, or use change starts from understanding instead of confusion.

That's the difference between civic engagement and civic empowerment. We don't just ask people what they think. We give them the knowledge and tools to shape their community with confidence.

Political integrity policy

ZoneCo believes in fair, accessible, and transparent government for all. ZoneCo does not make contributions to any political party, campaign, or candidate. We would rather influence our leaders through the power of big ideas. Read the policy

Although some ZoneCo professionals are also attorneys who may be separately engaged to provide legal representation in states where we are licensed to practice law, ZoneCo is not a law firm. ZoneCo professionals do not provide legal representation or services and are not engaged in the practice of law in any jurisdiction. Engaging ZoneCo does not form an attorney-client relationship and, as such, the protections of the attorney-client relationship do not apply. If you wish to create an attorney-client relationship, you are encouraged to contact an attorney of your choosing.
