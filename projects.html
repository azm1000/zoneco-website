Lexington, Kentucky

Imagine New Circle Road

New Circle Road is a notorious commercial beltway dominated by auto-oriented development. ZoneCo worked alongside a team of professionals to craft new village zoning for large parcels in B-3 zones along the corridor, part of a multi-year effort to reimagine this critical pathway through the Bluegrass.

Read the case study →
