Projects

Mission-driven, innovative, award-winning work.

In communities of all sizes in more than 20 states. A selection of recent projects, with links to the codes and diagnostic reports themselves. For every community we've served, see where we work.

Project spotlight

Case studies of seven recent rewrites.

Boston, Massachusetts

Article 80 modernization

Rewriting the 1996 article that governs Boston's large-project review, with a new system of impact mitigation and community benefits.

Read the case study →
Gaithersburg, Maryland

Retool Gaithersburg

The first comprehensive zoning rewrite since 1965, adopted 2024. Maryland Governor's Award and Maryland Sustainable Growth Award for Best Policy.

Read the case study →
Palm Beach, Florida

Once-in-a-generation rewrite

A 1930 code last overhauled in 1974, rebuilt for a 3.8-square-mile barrier island facing redevelopment pressure and sea-level rise.

Read the case study →
Vienna, Virginia

First overhaul since 1969

Adopted 2023. Calibrated standards on Maple Avenue, objective findings, and a code residents can read.

Read the case study →
Danville, Virginia

CODE Danville

A code untouched since 2004, rewritten in under twelve months to implement PLAN Danville. Adopted 2026.

Read the case study →
Springfield, Ohio

Comprehensive zoning rewrite

Patchwork regulations replaced with context-based districts, right-sized parking, and a path for missing-middle housing. Adopted 2025.

Read the case study →
Portsmouth, Ohio

One of the most equitable codes in America

A historic Ohio River city's code modernized for reinvestment, housing, and adaptive reuse.

Read the case study →
Huntsville, Alabama

Mixed-use village districts

Three new mixed-use village districts for Alabama's largest city, allowing auto-oriented corridors to become vibrant, livable villages within the city.

South Bend, Indiana

Form-based code

Winner of the 2021 Driehaus Form-Based Code Award from the Form-Based Code Institute and Smart Growth America. Read the ordinance.

Lexington, Kentucky

Imagine New Circle Road

New Circle Road is a notorious commercial beltway dominated by auto-oriented development. ZoneCo worked alongside a team of professionals to craft new village zoning for large parcels in B-3 zones along the corridor, part of a multi-year effort to reimagine this critical pathway through the Bluegrass. imaginelexington.com

Clermont County, Ohio

Pike with a Purpose, a CNU 32 Legacy Project

A three-day design charrette to envision a new village center along Ohio Pike (S.R. 125) in the former Village of Amelia, dissolved by its residents in 2019 and now part of Batavia and Pierce Townships.

Political integrity policy

ZoneCo believes in fair, accessible, and transparent government for all. ZoneCo does not make contributions to any political party, campaign, or candidate. We would rather influence our leaders through the power of big ideas. Read the policy

Although some ZoneCo professionals are also attorneys who may be separately engaged to provide legal representation in states where we are licensed to practice law, ZoneCo is not a law firm. ZoneCo professionals do not provide legal representation or services and are not engaged in the practice of law in any jurisdiction. Engaging ZoneCo does not form an attorney-client relationship and, as such, the protections of the attorney-client relationship do not apply. If you wish to create an attorney-client relationship, you are encouraged to contact an attorney of your choosing.
