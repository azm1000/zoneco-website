Background

Larisa’s career has focused on helping communities plan for growth and advance economic development. Her experience spans regional planning, municipal management, and airport business administration.

Prior to working with ZoneCo, Larisa served as Director of Business Administration at the Cincinnati/Northern Kentucky International Airport, where her responsibilities included economic development strategy, grant funding, international trade, and federal compliance. Previously, she served as Assistant City Manager in Newport, Kentucky, and Assistant City Manager for Development in Covington, Kentucky, where she led planning, economic, and community development activities. Earlier in her career, she served as Regional Planning Manager for the Ohio-Kentucky-Indiana Regional Council of Governments, working with local communities on transportation and planning issues across the three-state region.

Larisa is a certified planner through the American Institute of Certified Planners and held the Economic Development Finance Professional credential.

Leadership

Larisa has been active in regional boards and professional organizations throughout her career. Her service includes the Northern Kentucky Port Authority, Duke Energy Ohio & Kentucky Advisory Council, Covington Economic Development Authority, Northern Kentucky Chamber DEI Steering Committee, and the Trades NKY Board. She has also served on the National Planning Accreditation Board and the Kentucky League of Cities Board of Directors.

She has held leadership positions in both city management and planning organizations, including Vice President of the Northern Kentucky City and County Management Association and Conference Chair for the Kentucky City/County Management Association. With the Kentucky Chapter of the American Planning Association, she has served as President, Regional Conference Chair, Professional Development Officer, and a Legislative Committee member.

Her contributions have been recognized with the William Bowdy Lifetime Achievement Award for Planning, the Newport Leadership Award, the ASPA Greater Cincinnati Individual Contributor Award, CVG’s 2025 Above and Beyond – Lean into the Future Award, and the Legacy Next Generation Leader Award. She is a member of Leadership Northern Kentucky’s Class of 2018.

Political integrity policy

ZoneCo believes in fair, accessible, and transparent government for all. ZoneCo does not make contributions to any political party, campaign, or candidate. We would rather influence our leaders through the power of big ideas. Read the policy

Although some ZoneCo professionals are also attorneys who may be separately engaged to provide legal representation in states where we are licensed to practice law, ZoneCo is not a law firm. ZoneCo professionals do not provide legal representation or services and are not engaged in the practice of law in any jurisdiction. Engaging ZoneCo does not form an attorney-client relationship and, as such, the protections of the attorney-client relationship do not apply. If you wish to create an attorney-client relationship, you are encouraged to contact an attorney of your choosing.
