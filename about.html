About ZoneCo

Better codes make better places.

Founded in 2020 by city planner, lawyer, and former Chief Counsel for Land Use and Planning for the City of Cincinnati Sean S. Suder, ZoneCo has become one of the preeminent zoning code writing firms in America.

What we do, and why

ZoneCo is a national leader in zoning and land-use reform. We combine legal expertise, planning strategy, and design clarity to write zoning codes that are clear, consistent, user-friendly, equitable, and defensible.

We help communities of all sizes modernize outdated regulations, streamline development review, and align local rules with the community's vision. Through collaboration, data-driven analysis, and accessible design, we turn complex ordinances into tools that promote housing choice, economic vitality, and sustainable, inclusive growth.

Most American zoning codes were inherited, not designed. They were copied from a template a century ago and amended one grievance at a time. An inherited code doesn't need defending or demolishing. It needs a diagnosis, a calibration to the place it regulates, and a clear rewrite. That is the order we work in, for a reason.

Founded on three zoning principles

  1. Zoning should regulate only what needs to be regulated to advance the vision and promote public health, safety, and welfare.
  2. Zoning should respect both existing and desired development patterns.
  3. Zoning should be the implementation of a plan, not a barrier to achieving desired outcomes.

Every code we write starts here. If a regulation doesn't serve one of these, it doesn't belong in the code.

How we work

Diagnose

Every engagement starts with a zoning diagnostic: a plain-language report on what your code actually says, how it actually performs, and where it conflicts with what your community actually wants. Before anyone drafts a word.

Calibrate

Regulations are fitted to the real development patterns of each district, not copied from a template. Our Development Pattern Districting method treats the city as a network of distinct districts, each with its own regulatory habitat.

Codify

We write the code in plain language, with consolidated use tables, objective standards, published timelines, and graphics that explain the rules at a glance. Then we stay through adoption and beyond.

Human engineered. AI assists. Human judgment governs.

Many are talking about the impact of AI on zoning codes. We believe the impact has been positive, and we have embraced AI as a tool to make our code systems better. It increases speed and efficiency and can be a helpful thought partner. It cannot replace the judgment of ZoneCo's professionals, who together bring more than a century of experience to this field.

As we build out ZoneCo's AI structure, we use it for

  • Improving structural consistency
  • Accelerating diagnostics
  • Reducing drafting errors
  • Enhancing political intelligence
  • Systematizing modernization projects
  • Preserving institutional knowledge
